Autonomous Vehicle Navigation

In this project, I followed the online course (Introduction to Self-Driving Cars) to develop an autonomous vehicle navigation algorithm in CARLA simulator utilizing PID control for lateral and longitudinal error correction to ensuring smooth and accurate navigation. A physics-based approach was employed to model vehicle dynamics, capturing the real-world effects of movement and control inputs.
