About

I am an undergraduate senior robotics engineer at Franklin W. Olin College of Engineering, and extremely passionate about creating solutions through the lens and skillset of a roboticist.

As part of the Underwater Robotics Lab advised by Dr. Victoria Preston, my research dials in on perception for underwater ecosystems. My focus has been on developing a monocular and temporal 3D reconstruction pipeline to analyze highly dynamic soft corals. I also served as the Accumulator subteam lead on Olin’s Formula SAE team, where I oversaw a team of 10 engineers, managed the project timeline, and designed the 428V main battery pack.

My experience has allowed me to gain a holistic engineering background in an extremely fast-paced environment. I enjoy working in a hands-on and collaborative environment where the interdisciplinary aspect of robotics is emphasized and encouraged. I'd love to connect and chat!
